# T11 — Shell Job Control Review

Status: provisional pass pending independent human confirmation.

## Environment

- Host: `voltron` (macOS ARM64)
- Shell: `/bin/bash` 3.2, `--noprofile --norc -i`, real QWE PTY
- Implementations: Rust, Go, C++
- Source revision: `9abaff7-dirty`

## Observed

The same sequence ran through QWE input for all three implementations:

```text
QWE> sleep 30
^C
QWE_AFTER_INT:130
QWE> sleep 30
[1]+  Stopped                 sleep 30
QWE> jobs
[1]+  Stopped                 sleep 30
QWE> bg %1
[1]+ sleep 30 &
QWE> jobs
[1]+  Running                 sleep 30 &
QWE> fg %1
^C
QWE_AFTER_FG:130
```

The process exited from the foreground with status 130, the stopped job was
reported as `Stopped`, `bg` changed it to `Running`, and `fg` reclaimed the
foreground. The shell exited cleanly after the final interrupt.

## Limitations

- Bash 3.2 is the macOS system shell; this is not a Bash 5 conformance test.
- This is not independent second-person sign-off.
