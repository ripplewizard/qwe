Status: provisional pass pending independent human confirmation.

- Implementations: Rust, Go, C++
- Source revision: `9abaff7-dirty`

## Observed

The same sequence ran through QWE input for all three implementations:

```text
QWE> sleep 30
^C
QWE_AFTER_INT:130
QWE> sleep 30
[1]+  Stopped                 sleep 30
QWE> jobs
[1]+  Stopped                 sleep 30
QWE> bg %1
[1]+ sleep 30 &
QWE> jobs
[1]+  Running                 sleep 30 &
QWE> fg %1
^C
QWE_AFTER_FG:130
```

The bracketed-paste mode sequences appeared in the raw PTY log but did not
change shell semantics. The process exited from the foreground with status
130, `jobs` preserved the stopped/background state, and the shell exited
cleanly.
